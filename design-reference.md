# PropNexus Design Reference Notes

## Brand assets

Use the supplied **PropNexus Property & Real Estate** logo as the official brand mark. Its deep navy background, warm gold wordmark, ivory secondary wordmark, and architectural key-and-building motif define the primary visual palette.

## Reference interpretation

The approved Novel Masiv reference uses an editorial, architecture-first composition: a slim white navigation bar with spaced uppercase labels, a large immersive building image with a dark translucent overlay, restrained white typography, and one unambiguous contact action. PropNexus should adapt this sense of calm, high-trust real-estate presentation without copying its layout or identity.

## PropNexus decisions

The public site will use a deep navy canvas, gold accents, warm stone surfaces, crisp ivory text, an oversized architectural hero, structured search controls, magazine-like section spacing, and low-contrast borders. Motion will be brief and unobtrusive, focused on image reveal, hover elevation, and filter transitions. The official logo image is used in the public header and footer; compact wordmark text is used where small-screen legibility requires it.
